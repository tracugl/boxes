In the straight layout the frog microswitch sits beside the wire just under the turnout, screwed to a small ply block glued to the deck, and clear of the subway corridor. A small kink bent into the wire, 2 mm sideways towards the switch, sits under the lever near its hinge and glides along it to press it: nothing to solder. The kink is before the omega loop, so it moves exactly with the blades.

The mount plate is glued under the deck right below the turnout, with its slot over the deck's slot. The bellcrank hangs from it on an M3 screw. A short drop wire runs from the tiebar down through both slots into the bellcrank's input hole; the push-rod leaves from the output hole and runs along the track in its tube. The frog microswitch sits on the plate beside the input arm. Dimensions match the drawings: deck underside 74 mm, envelope top 66.8 mm, rod in its tube at 73 mm, servo 12 mm below the deck on spacers. The real throw is about 3.5 mm, so tick Exaggerate motion to see it clearly.
